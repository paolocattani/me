# Voice

Public copy for Paolo Cattani. If a draft fights this file, this file wins. When Paolo corrects a draft, encode the lesson here the same day.

## Who is speaking

Technical director. Builds software from the domain to the infrastructure. Writes as someone who shipped the thing, not as a commentator.

- Team action: *abbiamo*, *spostiamo*.
- Lesson: *ho imparato*, *ho bloccato*.
- Product: *il nostro ERP* or **Giova** (already on the home page). Never a customer name.

## Language

Italian first. English only when Paolo asks for a translation.

Sentence case. Short sentences. One idea each. Paragraphs of 1–3 sentences on LinkedIn, 2–4 on the blog.

No em dashes. Periods or commas. Straight quotes. No emoji. At most three hashtags, at the end of a LinkedIn post, never in the blog.

## What a piece must do

1. Open on a concrete claim or a thing that happened.
2. Move in stages that each repair a real previous problem. An intro post has no stages: why you write, then stop.
3. End on the lesson, not a summary and not a CTA.

The leading word for the first series is **drift**. Every step reduces drift. Chat is the last mile. If the source is dirty, the chat lies convincingly.

A silent LinkedIn profile starts with why Paolo writes. It is not a CV, not a thread index, and not the first technical piece. Do not write *torno a scrivere* or *come sapete*. He has never published here.

A colleague can be paraphrased. Do not put quotation marks around words we did not hear. Informal words from the pile (*fighe*) may stay: they are the fact, not decoration.

## What never goes in

Do not invent: metrics, external quotes, the date Notion started, vendor praise, customer names, production timing (including Q1 2027).

Do not put on LinkedIn: commit hashes, ticket ids, file paths, `STATUS.md`, `vector-seed`, `giova-docs`. The blog may name a path only if the sentence teaches a decision.

Do not write: *ancora*, *per ora*, *in un secondo momento*, *full agentic* as a flex, "Agree?", "thoughts?", carousel-caption broetry.

Cite a sister post only after it is public.

## Anti-tells (from unslop, kept because they match this voice)

- Cut: additionally, crucial, delve, landscape, pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, leverage, utilize.
- Do not write "not just X, but Y". State the point.
- Do not force groups of three.
- Do not bold labels that restate the line.
- Prefer *is* / *has* over *serves as* / *stands as*.
- Active voice. Name the actor.

## Tests before a draft is done

1. Would this sentence still be true if we stripped the product name? If yes and it names no fact, cut it.
2. Can every proper noun and number be pointed to in the pile (Notion page, transcript, this repo)? If not, delete it.
3. Read the first two LinkedIn lines only. Do they earn "see more"?
4. Read the last line. Is it the lesson, or a recap?
